RideLink Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026 · App: RideLink (app.ridelink) · Developer: Celso Narca Jr., Philippines

In short: your voice and shared music go straight from phone to phone between the riders in your ride. They are never recorded, stored, or sent to us or to any server. The only thing the app can send to us is an optional, anonymous problem report when something goes wrong, and you can turn that off.

What RideLink does

RideLink turns phones into a helmet intercom. One rider starts a ride and gets a 4-digit code; others join with that code. The phones then connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth. No internet connection or account is needed.

Your voice and music

  • Sound from your microphone is sent only to the other phones in your ride, while the ride is running.
  • If you choose to share music, only the sound of music playing on your phone is sent, only to the riders in your ride, and only until you stop sharing.
  • Voice and music are played and then discarded. RideLink never records, saves or uploads them.
  • The direct Wi-Fi and Bluetooth links between the phones are encrypted by Android.

The name you enter

The name you type ("What should riders call you?") is stored only on your phone and shown to the riders in your ride so they know who is talking. It is not sent to us.

Problem reports (optional)

When the app runs into an error, it can send a short report to the developer so the problem can be fixed. A report contains:

  • the phone model, Android version and RideLink version;
  • a random ID created when the app was installed (not linked to you, your phone number or your Google account);
  • the technical error and the connection steps that led to it.

Reports never contain your name, ride codes, location, contacts, voice or music. They are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a server run by the developer, are used only to fix bugs, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted after 12 months. You can turn reports off at any time in Settings → Send problem reports automatically.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Microphone: so riders can hear you.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to find and connect to the other riders' phones.
  • Location: Android requires this permission to search for nearby phones. RideLink never reads, stores or sends your location.
  • Notifications: so you can mute or leave the ride from the lock screen.
  • Screen and audio capture (only when you tap Share music): Android uses this to let RideLink hear the music playing on your phone. Only sound is captured; nothing on your screen is recorded or saved.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, messages or audio.

Children

RideLink is meant for adult riders and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
