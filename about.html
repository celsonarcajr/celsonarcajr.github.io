Editor first. Designer and tool-builder too.

I am a video editor based in Davao del Sur, in the Philippines. In 2026 I edited full-time for The Autonomous Leader, a business-education brand: a daily long-form talking-head episode and its companion shorts, plus the motion graphics that explain the ideas on screen.

I came to editing through design. Before that role I cut wedding films for Red White Blue Productions, made social content and banners for Topofny, and spent years in Photoshop producing custom pet portraits and print-ready product images. That is where the eye for detail comes from: skin tones, clean edges, type that still reads at phone size.

I also build my own tools. When a step in an edit repeats every day, I automate it — custom Premiere Pro panels for retake cutting and captions, a scheduler for YouTube uploads, and Shortsmith, a text-to-video app. I am looking for a full-time remote editing role, and I work comfortably in English on Philippine time (UTC+8).
