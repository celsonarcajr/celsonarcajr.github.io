AI-Built Motion Graphics

Explainer motion graphics for The Autonomous Leader, written as code with an AI assistant and mastered at 4K.

Year

2026

Client

The Autonomous Leader

Details

2026 — 4K masters and 9:16 cuts

Overview

For The Autonomous Leader's course modules and weekly videos I made the motion graphics a different way. Instead of keyframing every shot by hand, I directed an AI coding assistant, Claude Code, to write each shot as a Remotion composition: React code in which every line, box and label is drawn from the frame number.

My part was the direction. I chose which lines of the voiceover needed a picture, storyboarded the shots, set the house style (one teal accent, soft glows, rounded shapes, nothing cluttered) and reviewed every render against it. Each shot's timing comes from the transcript, so a diagram builds as the words arrive, and every visual beat gets a sound effect on the same frame.

Across nine batches (Module 0, Level 1, Level 2 and the weekly episodes) that came to more than 140 finished shots, delivered as 4K masters and as transparent ProRes overlays that dropped straight onto the Premiere timeline.

Nothing went out on the strength of a render log. Every batch was checked by script first: resolution, frame counts against the transcript timing, audio level, and for the overlays, how much of each frame is genuinely transparent.

How it works
Problem

Explainer videos need a lot of diagrams, and hand-animating twenty of them for every video does not scale week after week.

Solution

Treat each shot as code. An AI assistant writes the composition from a brief, I direct the look and the timing, and the finished renders are verified automatically before delivery.

Workflow
  1. Step 01

    Pick the lines of the transcript that need a picture

  2. Step 02

    Storyboard each shot as a still sheet

  3. Step 03

    Have the AI write the composition, timed to the spoken words

  4. Step 04

    Place a sound effect on every visual beat

  5. Step 05

    Render a quick 720p check and fix what the render shows

  6. Step 06

    Master at 4K or as a transparent overlay, then verify frames, audio and alpha
