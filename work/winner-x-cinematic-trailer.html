2026 — cinematic trailer

Overview

A cinematic trailer for the Honda Winner X: a rider, a red Winner X and an empty concrete road winding through the rice fields under a hazy sky.

Every shot is mine: I filmed all of it myself — the ground shots on my mirrorless cameras and the aerials on my drone — and I did the editing too.

It moves between drone shots that follow the bike through the fields and close details — a hand on the switches, the headlight up close, the rider walking up to the bike — and it ends on the rider riding off into the haze.

Edited and finished at 1080p.
